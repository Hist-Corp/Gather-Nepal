Nepal's Living Heritage

Where tradition meets modern gathering

Nepal's cultural heritage spans thousands of years — from Newari festivals in the Kathmandu Valley to Thangka painting workshops in Bhaktapur. Gather Nepal connects you with events that honor these traditions while building new community experiences.

Traditional Arts

Thangka painting, wood carving, pottery, and Newari craft workshops led by master artisans.

Live Music & Dance

From classical Nepali ragas to contemporary fusion — experience music in historic venues.

Sacred Spaces

Events held in ancient courtyards, temples, and heritage sites across the Kathmandu Valley.

Cultural Festivals

Dashain, Tihar, Holi, and local jatra celebrations — join the festivals that define Nepal.
